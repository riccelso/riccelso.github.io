Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Hermes Agent is a personal automation tool. This policy describes how the app handles Google user data.

Data access

The app requests the Google OAuth scope drive.file only. This scope limits access to files and folders that the app itself creates. The app cannot list, read, or modify any other files in the user's Google Drive.

Data use

  • Google Drive data is accessed solely to store and serve media files for the app owner's personal use.
  • No Google user data is sold, shared, or transferred to third parties.
  • No Google user data is used for advertising or marketing purposes.
  • No data is used to train machine-learning or AI models.

Data storage

The app stores authentication tokens required to maintain its access. Tokens are kept confidential and are never displayed or transferred. The app does not store file contents outside the user's own Google Drive.
